Why subsampling was used
------------------------
The candidate models use NNDSVDa initialisation, which is substantially deterministic for an unchanged input matrix. Repeatedly fitting the identical corpus would therefore provide little evidence about topic robustness. Repeated 80% corpus subsampling tests whether similar topics are recovered when corpus composition changes.
